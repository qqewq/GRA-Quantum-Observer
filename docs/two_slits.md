# Double-Slit Experiment and Foam

In the classic double-slit experiment:

- An electron fired at a barrier with two slits produces:
  - an **interference pattern** when no one measures which slit it went through,
  - a **particle-like pattern** (two bright bands) when a detector records "which-path" information.

Interpretation:

- Without observation:
  - the electron behaves like a **wave**, exploring multiple paths.
- With observation:
  - the electron behaves like a **particle**, choosing one path.

GRA analogy:

- Without nullification N:
  - a cognitive state Ψ is in a **high-foam** regime:
    - many conflicting arguments,
    - many possible "stories" of what is real,
    - no clear decision.
- With nullification N:
  - Ψ is forced into a **low-foam** configuration:
    - one dominant narrative,
    - internal consistency,
    - subjective certainty.

Thus:

> **Observation in quantum physics ≈ Nullification in GRA.**  
> Both reduce a rich superposition of possibilities to a single experienced reality.

This does not claim that foam is literally quantum amplitude.  
It claims that **the functional role** is similar: collapsing multiplicity into one branch.
