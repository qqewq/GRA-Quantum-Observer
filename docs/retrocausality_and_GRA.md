# Retrocausality and GRA

In quantum foundations, **retrocausality** refers to models where:

- future measurement settings influence past events,
- without leading to paradoxes (because "past events" are defined relationally).

In GRA:

- The **history of foam Φ** is not an immutable log.
- Each new nullification step N:
  - re-evaluates which arguments matter,
  - restructures which branches are kept as "my history",
  - potentially erases or demotes past branches.

So:

> When a cognitive system applies N, it is effectively rewriting its own past — not in the physical sense, but in the sense of which stories remain real for the subject.

Examples:

- A person reinterprets a trauma after therapy:
  - the physical past is unchanged,
  - but the **cognitive past** is reorganized,
  - the foam Φ around that event drops.

GRA-Quantum-Observer formalizes this:

- **Retrocausality = retroactive reclassification of foam**.
- The observer (N) changes not only future actions, but also:
  - which parts of the past remain active in the cognitive universe,
  - and which fall into oblivion.

Physical retrocausality remains controversial.  
Cognitive retrocausality in GRA is natural and central.
